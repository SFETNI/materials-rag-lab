## NIST material

The companion dataset includes resources from the NIST IN718 dataset identified by `ark:/88434/mds2-3044` and DOI `10.18434/mds2-3044`. NIST source and license information is retained in the dataset source and rights registries. Users should acknowledge NIST and follow the applicable NIST terms.

## NASA material

NASA technical reports are handled according to the rights determination recorded by NASA NTRS for each report. Public distribution alone is not treated as a redistribution license. Source identifiers and rights evidence are retained in the dataset rights registry.

NASA NTRS `20230010244` is treated conservatively as reference-only and is not redistributed in the companion dataset.

NASA NTRS `20180005513` is a different boundary: the original active-media PDF container is excluded from packaging, but its text-derived canonical evidence remains in the redistributable projection. This packaging record does not add to the 25 retrieval units excluded from the two reference-only text sources.

## Publisher-controlled article

Kafka et al., 2023, *International Journal of Fatigue*, DOI `10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2023.107872`, is reference-only. The article PDF and its long-text derivatives are not redistributed in the companion dataset. Obtain it through an authorized publisher or institutional route.
